QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to common questions from clients planning their financial future.

Which services can I discuss with Adrita Bima Kendra?

You can discuss life, health and motor insurance, mutual funds, retirement planning, income-tax returns, GST services, loans and goal-based financial planning.

Do I need to book an appointment before visiting or calling?

An appointment helps us understand your requirements and prepare better, but you can also contact us directly through phone or WhatsApp for an initial discussion.

Can you help me plan for my child’s education or my retirement?

Yes. We can help you identify your target, time horizon and regular savings capacity, then explain suitable protection and investment options for your consideration.

Will I receive support after purchasing a policy or starting an investment?

Yes. We remain available for service requests, renewals, basic documentation support and periodic plan reviews as your needs change.

What should I bring for a financial-planning discussion?

Bring any existing policy or investment details, your broad monthly budget, key family responsibilities and the goals you would like to plan for. We will guide you on any additional documents required.
