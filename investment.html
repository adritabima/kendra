INVESTMENT OPTIONS

Choose the Right Type for the Right Goal

Every option has different return potential, risk, liquidity, lock-in and tax treatment.

LIC & Life Insurance

Insurance primarily provides financial protection. Common categories include term, endowment, whole-life, annuity/pension and ULIP plans.

Purpose: protection and long-term planning

Mutual Funds & SIPs

SIPs invest regularly in mutual-fund schemes. Fund values can rise or fall with markets; select funds according to your goal and risk profile.

Purpose: market-linked long-term growth

NPS

The National Pension System is a retirement-focused option with asset-allocation choices and withdrawal rules set by the scheme.

Purpose: retirement corpus

Fixed Deposits

Bank and company FDs generally offer a stated interest rate for a fixed period, subject to issuer terms and taxation.

Purpose: capital stability and known tenure

PPF & EPF

Long-term, government-backed or employment-linked savings options with rules on contributions, tenure and withdrawals.

Purpose: disciplined long-term savings

Gold & Bonds

Gold, government securities and other bonds may be used for diversification, income or capital-preservation needs.

Purpose: diversification and stability

Equity Investments

Equity can offer long-term growth potential, but prices fluctuate and capital value is not guaranteed.

Purpose: higher-risk long-term growth

ULIPs

ULIPs combine insurance and market-linked investment components. Compare charges, lock-in, allocation and policy features carefully.

Purpose: insurance plus market exposure
LIC PRODUCT CATEGORIES

Understand LIC Plans Before You Choose

Plan availability and features can change. Always verify the latest official brochure, benefits, exclusions and policy terms.

Protection-Oriented Plans

Term and other protection-focused policies are designed around life-cover needs. The right cover depends on dependants, income, loans and financial responsibilities.

Savings & Endowment Plans

These plans combine protection with a structured savings element. Assess premium affordability, tenure, maturity benefits and surrender rules.

Pension & Annuity Plans

Pension and annuity products may support retirement-income planning. Understand deferment, annuity choices, payout options and taxation.

ULIPs

ULIPs are insurance products with market-linked funds. Review the mandatory lock-in, fund choices, charges and investment risk before investing.

For the current LIC product list and official brochures, visit LIC of India.

MUTUAL FUNDS & SIPs

Why SIPs Can Suit Some Long-Term Goals

A SIP is not universally better than an FD, insurance plan or lump-sum investment. It may be suitable when you have a long horizon, can accept market risk and want to invest regularly.

SIP in Mutual Funds

  • Regular, disciplined investing
  • Market-linked value; returns are not guaranteed
  • May suit long-term goals and risk-taking capacity
  • Fund categories include equity, debt, hybrid and others
  • Review risk level, expenses, goal and time horizon

Fixed Deposit

  • Usually offers stated interest for a selected tenure
  • May suit short- to medium-term stability needs
  • Interest is generally taxable as per applicable rules
  • Premature withdrawal rules and penalties can apply
  • Does not carry equity-market volatility
SIP and FD serve different purposes. Choose based on goal, liquidity needs, risk tolerance, tenure and tax position—not only on past returns.

RETIREMENT PLANNING

NPS, Pension Planning & Retirement Income

Retirement planning looks at future expenses, inflation, time remaining, contribution capacity and desired income after retirement.

NPS Overview

NPS is a retirement-focused system with contribution, asset-allocation and withdrawal rules. It offers market-linked investment choices within the scheme framework.

Asset Allocation

Allocation between equity, corporate debt and government securities affects potential growth and volatility. A suitable mix depends on age and risk comfort.

Income Planning

Plan for essential expenses, healthcare, inflation and other income sources, not only the final retirement corpus.

Know the Rules

Review current NPS rules, charges, withdrawal conditions and tax implications before contributing or changing allocations.

FAQs

Common Investment Questions

Which investment is best?

No single investment is best for everyone. The suitable option depends on your goal, risk tolerance, time horizon, liquidity needs and financial situation.

Should I buy insurance only for investment returns?

Insurance should first be considered for protection needs. Compare product features, costs, benefits and your protection requirement before making a decision.

Are mutual-fund SIP returns guaranteed?

No. Mutual-fund investments are subject to market risks, and returns can fluctuate. Past performance does not guarantee future performance.

Can I invest in SIP and FD together?

Yes, some investors use different options for different goals. The mix should reflect your liquidity, stability and growth needs.

What is a ULIP lock-in?

ULIPs have a mandatory lock-in period under applicable rules. Read the policy brochure for the current lock-in, charges and surrender conditions.

Is NPS only for salaried people?

NPS is available to eligible individuals subject to applicable rules. Review the latest official eligibility and account-opening requirements.
