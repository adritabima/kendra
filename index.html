FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which insurance and financial services do you provide?

We assist with life, health and motor insurance, mutual funds, SIPs, loans and financial planning.

How do I choose the right insurance policy?

Our team first understands your family, income, goals and budget, then explains suitable options clearly.

Can I start a SIP with a small monthly amount?

Yes. The suitable amount depends on your goals and financial situation; contact us for guidance.

How can I book a free consultation?

Use any WhatsApp button on this page or send the contact form. Our team will respond as soon as possible.

What is the difference between life insurance and health insurance?

Life insurance helps protect your family financially in case of an unfortunate event, while health insurance helps cover eligible medical treatment expenses.

Can I get health insurance for my parents?

Yes. Suitable plans depend on their age, medical history, coverage need and insurer terms. We can help you understand the available options.

What documents are usually needed for an insurance policy?

Requirements vary by policy, but may include identity proof, address proof, PAN card, bank details and medical information where applicable.

How does a mutual fund SIP work?

A SIP invests a chosen amount at regular intervals. It can help build disciplined long-term investing, but returns depend on market performance and are not guaranteed.

Can I stop or change my SIP amount later?

In many cases, yes. The process depends on the scheme and platform. Contact us before making changes so you understand the possible effect on your goals.

Do you help with policy renewal and claim guidance?

Yes. We can assist with renewal reminders and guide you through the information and documents generally required for a claim process.

Can you help me understand loan options?

Yes. We can explain suitable loan options based on your requirement and help you understand key terms, eligibility and documentation.

Is there a fee for the first consultation?

Use the WhatsApp consultation button to share your requirement. Our team will explain the available guidance and any applicable service details clearly.
